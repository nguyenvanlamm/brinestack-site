How to play

  1. Read the sonar.The SONAR panel counts creatures by the height they attack: high, mid, face and low.
  2. Drag a deck to the threat.Put the weapon that reaches that height where the hits land. Keep a hurt deck in the middle so it mends.
  3. Crack every nest.Clear the dive, earn stars and shells, then refit your decks at the Dock.

Questions

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads and no ad networks in the game.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game contacts the App Store only to show prices, make purchases and restore them.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases on any device with the same Apple account.

What do the boosters do?

A Depth Charge clears every creature in reach of the stack. A Second Wind rebuilds your decks once after a Hull Breach. You earn some by playing; packs are optional.
