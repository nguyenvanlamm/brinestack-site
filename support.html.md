## How to play

Your stack of decks is towed along the sea floor toward coral nests, and each deck fires on its own. Creatures attack by height: crabs and hermits hit the bottom deck, sea stars climb the stack, jellyfish sink onto the top deck and eels strike the middle. Drag a deck up or down to put the weapon that reaches a height where the hits land; decks in the middle mend when nothing hits them. Tap a deck to Patch or Overdrive it with pearls. With VoiceOver, each deck offers "Move up" and "Move down" actions. Crack every nest to clear the dive, then spend shells at the Dock.

## Purchases

The Shallows (dives 1–12) is free. All Seas is a one-time purchase that opens Kelp Deep and the Trench. Supply Crate and Supply Hold are optional booster packs. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open *Settings → Restore Purchases*.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
