# Brinestack, a tactical dive game for iPhone. Drag one deck. Save the whole stack.

**Coming soon to the App Store.**

Brinestack is a short, tactical dive. Sea creatures hit your towed stack at four heights, and you move the right weapon to meet them while the dive is running.

12 dives free · no ads · no tracking · plays offline

## Your tower never had to stay put

- **Builds lock at the start.** Most tower games freeze your setup before the wave arrives. When the wave changes shape, you watch.
- **The sea hits every height.** Jellyfish sting the top deck while your Flak sits at the bottom. A sea star climbs past a Clamp two slots too low.
- **Move any deck, any time.** Drag a deck to any slot mid-dive. Decks in the middle mend while nothing hits them, so where you put them matters twice.

Who attacks which height:

- **High:** jellyfish sink onto the top deck
- **Mid:** eels strike the middle deck
- **Face:** sea stars climb the stack
- **Low:** crabs and hermits hit the bottom deck

## What you get

- **Six weapons, each with a reach.** Harpoon, Clamp, Flak, Mortar, Coil and Fan each cover only some heights. Tap a deck to Patch it or Overdrive it with pearls.
- **36 dives, three seas.** The Shallows, Kelp Deep and the Trench, each deeper and busier. Between dives, the Dock turns shells into Refits, Quick Clamps and a Pearl Pouch.
- **Fair by design.** No ads, no account, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone, and it plays offline.

## How to play

1. **Read the sonar.** The SONAR panel counts creatures by the height they attack: high, mid, face and low.
2. **Drag a deck to the threat.** Put the weapon that reaches that height where the hits land. Keep a hurt deck in the middle so it mends.
3. **Crack every nest.** Clear the dive, earn stars and shells, then refit your decks at the Dock.

## Questions

- **What does it cost?** The Shallows, dives 1–12, are free. All Seas is a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) that opens Kelp Deep and the Trench: 24 more dives. Supply Crate ($0.99) and Supply Hold ($2.99) are optional booster packs.
- **Are there ads?** No. There are no ads and no ad networks in the game.
- **Does it need the internet?** No. The game contacts the App Store only to show prices, make purchases and restore them.
- **Where is my progress saved?** On your device only. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases on any device with the same Apple account. See the [privacy policy](https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/brinestack-site/privacy.html.md).
- **What do the boosters do?** A Depth Charge clears every creature in reach of the stack. A Second Wind rebuilds your decks once after a Hull Breach. You earn some by playing; packs are optional.
- **Which devices does it run on?** iPhone, with iOS 15 or later. Need help? Visit [support](https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/brinestack-site/support.html.md).
